# Software Requirements Specification (SRS): Fazna Solar Energy Corporate Website

This document outlines the requirements for a new corporate website for Fazna Solar Energy LLC. The website will serve as a digital showcase for the company's EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) services in the solar energy sector, targeting potential B2B clients and partners. The SRS follows best practices for structure and clarity .

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the functional and non-functional requirements for the development of Fazna Solar Energy's official website. This SRS will guide designers, developers, and stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle .

### 1.2 Scope
The website will be a marketing and informational platform, not a transactional system. It will present the company's profile, expertise, project portfolio, management team, certifications, and contact information. The system will be accessible via desktop and mobile browsers.

### 1.3 Definitions
*   **EPC:** Engineering, Procurement, and Construction.
*   **B2B:** Business-to-Business.
*   **UI/UX:** User Interface/User Experience.
*   **CMS:** Content Management System.

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product Perspective
The website is a standalone application that will replace or supplement the company's current digital presence. It will integrate with standard web analytics tools.

### 2.2 Product Functions
The core functions include displaying static content (text, images, videos), providing contact forms, and showcasing project case studies.

### 2.3 User Characteristics
The primary users are:
*   **Potential Clients:** Large corporations, government entities, and developers seeking EPC contractors for solar projects.
*   **Industry Partners:** Suppliers, technology providers, and investors.
*   **Job Seekers:** Professionals looking for opportunities in the renewable energy sector.
*   **General Public:** Individuals seeking information about the company.

### 2.4 Constraints
*   The website must reflect the company's ISO certifications (9001, 14001, 45001).
*   Design must align with the professional and innovative image of a regional leader in renewable energy .
*   Content must be easily updatable by non-technical staff via a CMS.

### 2.5 Assumptions and Dependencies
*   High-quality images and videos from past projects (e.g., Sweihan, Al Dafra, Sakkakah) will be provided.
*   Company management (Syed Ibrahim Asif, Chittibabu Lakkum) will provide approved biographies and photos.
*   The website will be hosted on a reliable, preferably green, hosting provider .

## 3. Specific Requirements

### 3.1 Functional Requirements

#### 3.1.1 User Interface (UI) & User Experience (UX)
The website design should incorporate modern trends for 2025, particularly those relevant to the corporate and renewable energy sectors . Key UI/UX principles include:
*   **Minimalist and Clean Design:** A clutter-free interface that emphasizes content and professionalism .
*   **Mobile-First Design:** The site must be fully responsive and provide an optimal experience on all device sizes .
*   **Sustainable Web Design:** Implement practices like image compression and efficient coding to reduce the site's digital carbon footprint, aligning with the company's core business .
*   **Clear Navigation:** Intuitive menu structure allowing users to find information within 1-2 clicks.
*   **Compelling Visuals:** High-resolution images and potentially 3D elements or interactive project maps to showcase large-scale projects .
*   **Clear Calls-to-Action (CTAs):** Strategically placed buttons (e.g., "Contact Us," "View Our Projects," "Request a Proposal") to guide user behavior .

#### 3.1.2 Page Structure and Content
The website shall include the following essential pages for a B2B solar EPC contractor :

*   **Home Page:**
    *   Hero section with a powerful headline and sub-headline communicating Fazna's value proposition as a regional EPC leader.
    *   Brief company introduction and mission statement.
    *   Key statistics or highlights (e.g., "2+ GW Installed," "Projects Across ME & India").
    *   Featured project thumbnails.
    *   Quick links to major sections (Projects, Services, About).
    *   Contact CTA.

*   **About Us:**
    *   Company history and establishment (Founded 2022, Dubai).
    *   Detailed "Our Mission" and "Our Values" (Team spirit, excellence, innovation, adaptability, integrity).
    *   Company management profiles with photos and bios for Syed Ibrahim Asif (Managing Director) and Chittibabu Lakkum (Technical Director), highlighting their expertise in renewable energy development .
    *   Display of ISO certifications (9001, 14001, 45001) with downloadable certificates or verification links.
    *   Information about the parent group and subsidiary (Aye Totes Pvt Ltd, India).

*   **Services:**
    *   Detailed description of EPC services: Trackers, Modules, PV Tables, Robot Installation, Earthing & Alignment.
    *   Breakdown of sectors: Ground-mounted, Rooftop, Industrial Generator & Storage, Temporary Power, Long-Term Leasing.
    *   Explanation of the construction methodology (referencing the 10-step process from the PDF).
    *   Showcase of technical capabilities, including manpower, machinery, tools, and site facilities (presented in an engaging, visual way, not just tables).

*   **Projects / Portfolio:**
    *   Dedicated section for major project references.
    *   Case studies for key projects (Sweihan Zones 1&4, Sweihan Zone 3B, Saudi Sakkakah, Oman PDO Amin, Al Dafra PV2, DEWA VI, Khazna Data Centre) including:
        *   Project name, location, and capacity (MW).
        *   Scope of work performed by Fazna.
        *   High-quality site photos and videos.
        *   Client testimonials or appreciation letters (if available).
        *   Challenges and solutions.
    *   Separate section or filter for projects executed in India under "Sap Structures."

*   **Resources / Downloads (Optional but Recommended):**
    *   Downloadable company profile (PDF).
    *   Brochures for specific services.
    *   Technical specifications or whitepapers.

*   **Contact Us:**
    *   Contact form for general inquiries.
    *   Direct contact information for Dubai (Mr. Syed Ibrahim Asif) and India (Mr. Chittibabu Lakkum) offices, including phone numbers and emails.
    *   Interactive map showing the Dubai office location (Plot No. 119-0, Dubai Investment Park First).
    *   Links to social media profiles (if applicable).

*   **Footer:**
    *   Sitemap links.
    *   Legal information (Copyright, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use).
    *   Reiteration of contact info and social links.

### 3.2 Non-Functional Requirements

*   **Performance:** The website must load within 3 seconds on average. Images and assets must be optimized .
*   **Usability:** The interface must be intuitive and easy to navigate for users of varying technical skill levels .
*   **Reliability:** The website must have an uptime of 99.9%.
*   **Security:** The site must implement HTTPS, protect against common web vulnerabilities (e.g., XSS, SQL injection), and ensure contact form data is handled securely.
*   **Maintainability:** The codebase must follow consistent coding standards and best practices . The CMS must allow authorized staff to easily update text, images, and project case studies.
*   **Accessibility:** The website should comply with WCAG 2.1 AA standards to ensure accessibility for users with disabilities .
